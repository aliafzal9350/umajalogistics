# Umaja revenue and AdSense plan

## Start with freight bookings

The November 9 AdSense screenshot shows verified ownership and a low-value-content decision. It gives no page-count, site-age or traffic target. Improve the experience for a shipper deciding whether to request a quote, then review the live evidence before resubmitting. [Google AdSense site guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563)

1. **JFK air cargo trucking** targets recovery, export tenders and handler transfers. Give shippers the release and timing details needed before dispatch.
2. **Liftgate delivery** targets receivers without a dock. Explain how access, loaded dimensions and the combined load on the gate affect the move.
3. **Dedicated box truck freight** targets shippers comparing direct and shared freight. Connect the service page to the existing pallet calculator and the new shipment brief.

The November 6 OpenSEO report estimated 110 US monthly searches for “box truck delivery service.” Use that to inform the dedicated service page. Assess real traffic and search queries in Search Console. Publish a lane page after dispatch confirms repeat coverage and can describe a lane-specific pickup or receiving decision. [OpenSEO research](https://app.openseo.so/p/0e0ed5c9-675e-4620-a7da-763c14884772/reports/df4d449d-b962-4282-9bcd-df538986cdf7) · [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#doorway-abuse)

## Measure and improve weekly

- In Search Console, compare organic clicks, impressions and queries by service page. Inspect the new URLs and confirm Google can index them.
- The brief emits `quote_brief_copied`, `quote_email_clicked` and `quote_call_clicked` into the existing GTM data layer without form fields. Configure corresponding Custom Event triggers and GA4 event tags in Umaja's container, then test with Tag Assistant and DebugView before relying on counts. An email click indicates an attempt to open a draft. A call click records a tap.
- Have dispatch record actual web inquiries, qualified loads, quotes, bookings and contribution margin after direct costs. Ask callers how they found Umaja. Use that log to see which services earn money and where booking falls through.
- Publish a real, approved shipment walkthrough with customer details removed and a current equipment photo or measured specification. Use recurring dispatch questions to decide what to add next.

## Before requesting another AdSense review

Confirm the changed pages are live and discoverable, the calculator and shipment brief work on mobile, and the most important content is indexed. Review genuine search and tool use together with the original evidence added since the rejection. Check the published consent message and keep ad placements on substantive content, clear of tool and quote actions. Review is a separate Google decision. [Google publisher content policy](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11112688)
